FAQ

Frequently asked questions

 

Who is Nirbhay Awaaz for?
Creators making Reels, YouTube Shorts, videos, ads, courses and podcasts who need a voiceover quickly.
How do emotion tags work?
Add tags such as [laughs], [whispers] or [sighs] in your script and the voice will deliver that emotion. Add [pause 10s] to insert a 10-second silence.
Can I generate Hindi voiceovers?
Yes. Natural Hindi and English voices are available, and you can pick the voice that fits your content.
How does voice cloning work?
Upload a clear audio sample and the system creates a clone of that voice. Once it is ready, you can generate new text in that voice.
Can I clone someone else's voice?
No. Only clone a voice you own or have clear written permission to use.
Can I use the audio for Reels and Shorts?
Yes. Download the audio and use it in any video editor. It works well for vertical 9:16 formats.
What does lifetime access include?
All available voices, Hindi and English, and voice cloning. Exact terms will be listed in the pricing section.
